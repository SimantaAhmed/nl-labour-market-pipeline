Download the data

Free to use for coursework, research and reporting. Files are regenerated with every Statistics Canada release. Blank values are months Statistics Canada suppressed.

  • nl_labour_monthly.csv Every monthly value since 1976 in long format: NL and Canada headline series plus NL employment by industry. Columns: month, geography, indicator, industry, sector, unit, value.
  • nl_vs_canada_rates.csv One row per month with NL and Canada unemployment, participation and employment rates side by side. Easiest file to open in Excel.
  • nl_industry_employment.csv NL employment by industry as 12-month averages, with 1-year and 10-year change. The figures behind the chart above.
  • revisions.csv Every value Statistics Canada changed after this pipeline first loaded it, with the old and new value.

Rates are percentages. Counts are thousands of people. All series are seasonally adjusted, population aged 15 and over.
